# Peter Pan
Peter Pan is a magical boy who never grows up. He is a fictional character created by the writer J. M. Barrie in 1904.

He lives on the magical island of Neverland, with his fairy friend Tinker Bell and a group of children called the Lost Boys. 
He goes on exciting adventures, flies through the sky, and fights against pirates, especially the evil Captain Hook.

He usually wears clothes made of green leaves, carries a small dagger, and uses fairy dust to fly. 
Although he is very famous, he is a character from books and plays, not a person who really existed.
