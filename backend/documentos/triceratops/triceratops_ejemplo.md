# Triceratops

The Triceratops was a herbivorous dinosaur, which means it only ate plants. It fed
on ferns, leaves and low-growing plants from the Cretaceous period. It had a beak
similar to a parrot's that it used to cut tough plants before chewing them.

It had three horns on its face and a large bony frill behind its head. It used them
to defend itself and to show off, not to hunt, because it was not a hunter.

The Triceratops lived at the end of the Cretaceous period, about 66 million years ago,
in what is now North America. It was big: about 9 meters long and it weighed as much
as a large elephant.

It shared its time with the Tyrannosaurus Rex, which was its greatest danger. Its horns
helped it defend itself from the T-Rex.
