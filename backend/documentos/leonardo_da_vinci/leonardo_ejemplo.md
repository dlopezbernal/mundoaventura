# Leonardo da Vinci

Leonardo da Vinci was an artist and inventor from the Renaissance. He was born in 1452
in the town of Vinci, in Italy, and he died in 1519.

He painted very famous works, such as the Mona Lisa (La Gioconda) and The Last Supper.
He filled notebooks with inventions ahead of his time: flying machines, a kind of
helicopter, and even a parachute.

He loved studying nature: water, light, birds and the human body. For him, art and science
went together. He wrote many notes backwards, in mirror writing, which is read with a
mirror. He was very curious and always asked himself questions about how everything he saw
worked.
