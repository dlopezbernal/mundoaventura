# Sherlock Holmes

Sherlock Holmes is a detective. He is a fictional character created by the writer Arthur
Conan Doyle in 1887.

He lives in London, at 221B Baker Street, with his great friend and assistant, Doctor
Watson. He solves mysteries using observation and deduction: he notices small details that
others miss and draws conclusions.

He usually uses a magnifying glass to examine clues, a deerstalker hunting cap, and sometimes
a pipe. Although he is very famous, he is a character from books, not a person who really
existed.
