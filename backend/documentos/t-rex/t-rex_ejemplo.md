# Tyrannosaurus Rex

The Tyrannosaurus Rex was a carnivorous dinosaur, which means it ate meat. It was one
of the largest predators that ever lived on land.

It had huge teeth, some as big as a banana, and one of the strongest bites of any animal.
Its arms were very small and had two fingers, but its hind legs were strong and large.
It had an extraordinary sense of smell that helped it find food from far away.

The T-Rex lived at the end of the Cretaceous period, about 66 million years ago, in North
America. It was about 12 meters long and its head reached very high. One of its neighbors
was the Triceratops.
